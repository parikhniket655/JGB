EXECUTIVE MEMORANDUM

TO: JBG Management Team | FROM: Business Strategy Associate | DATE: August 12, 2026

SUBJECT: JBG Portfolio Growth Engine (GPE) — Capital Allocation & Synergetic Growth Strategy

Context & Strategic Challenge

Jithvar Business Group (JBG) operates a highly diversified portfolio across Technology Consulting (JCS), Renewable Energy (Nature Grid), Recruitment & Workforce Solutions (Aequalis Global), and SaaS/New Ventures. While each business shows strong individual potential, JBG faces classic constraints: limited capital, management attention, and execution bandwidth. The fundamental strategic challenge is not merely listing new business ideas, but building a systematic, repeatable engine to decide where to grow, which opportunities to pursue, and what to execute first.

Core Recommendation: The JBG Portfolio Growth Engine (GPE)

Rather than operating these business units as independent silos, JBG must transition to a unified, capability-driven platform. We propose the JBG Portfolio Growth Engine (GPE), a structured methodology that optimizes core operations, drives cross-business synergies, and objectively evaluates opportunities using a quantitative prioritization scorecard.

Job 1: Grow What Exists (Core Optimization)

  • JCS: Pivot from low-margin custom software development to international IT managed services, CRM/ERP integrations, and cloud migrations. This secures high-margin retainers.
  • Nature Grid: Shift focus from capital-intensive residential installations to Commercial & Industrial (C&I) solar and high-margin, long-term Operation & Maintenance (O&M) contracts.
  • Aequalis Global: Expand from transactional executive search into high-frequency contract staffing and specialized IT recruitment, creating a predictable, recurring cash flow engine.

Job 2: Find What’s Next (Cross-Business Synergies)

  • Recruitment SaaS (HR Tech): Combine Aequalis Global’s domain expertise and client networks with JCS’s software engineering to build a vertical recruitment automation platform.
  • Energy Management Analytics: Combine Nature Grid’s solar site installations with JCS's technical capabilities to build an IoT-driven solar asset monitoring and predictive maintenance SaaS.
  • Consulting Productization: Package repeatable consulting methodologies from JCS into software-as-a-service (SaaS) templates for mid-market clients, transitioning from project-based billing to scalable subscription revenue.

The Opportunity Prioritization Scorecard

To eliminate emotional capital allocation, JBG must screen all growth initiatives through a standardized, multi-factor scorecard: Market Attractiveness (20%), Revenue Potential (20%), Strategic Fit (15%), JBG Capability Fit (15%), Competitive Intensity (10%), Ease/Speed of Execution (10%), Capital Required (5%), and Execution/Operational Risk (5%). This transforms strategic debate from intuitive guesswork into objective commercial ranking.

90-Day Execution Roadmap

  • Days 1–30 (Diagnose): Review baseline financial and capability metrics for existing business lines. Establish the JBG Business Health Dashboard.
  • Days 31–60 (Discover): Populate the growth opportunity pipeline. Assess and score opportunities using the Prioritization Scorecard, narrowing the longlist from 20+ to 3 priority pilots.
  • Days 61–90 (Validate): Conduct customer discovery, test pricing assumptions, and launch minimum viable pilots (MVPs) for the top-ranked opportunities.

Key Metrics of Success

JBG should measure success across a hierarchy of KPIs: Portfolio-level ROIC and revenue growth, Initiative-level customer acquisition cost (CAC) and payback period, and Innovation-level metrics—most notably the % of projects killed early—to ensure capital discipline.

SUPPORTING STRATEGIC ANALYSIS

Detailed Formulas, Synergy Models, and Risk Management Frameworks

1. Prioritization Scorecard Mathematics

To ensure objectivity, the Opportunity Score (Sopp) is calculated as a weighted sum of individual scores across 8 factors. Each factor is scored on a scale of 1 to 10:

Sopp = wmarket·smarket + wrevenue·srevenue + wcapability·scapability + wstrategic·sstrategic + wcompetition·scompetition + wexecution·sexecution + wcapital·scapital + wrisk·srisk

Where wi is the weight of factor i (such that Σ wi = 100%) and si is the raw score of factor i (on a 1–10 scale).

Scorecard Weights and Definitions:

  • Market Attractiveness (20%): Evaluates the Total Addressable Market (TAM), served addressable market (SAM), and CAGR.
  • Revenue Potential (20%): Assesses pricing power, customer willingness to pay, and recurring nature of revenue (subscriptions/retainers).
  • JBG Capability Fit (15%): Measures leverage of existing JCS software developers, Aequalis recruiter networks, and Nature Grid solar technicians.
  • Strategic Fit (15%): Evaluates alignment with the JBG brand, portfolio diversification, and long-term enterprise valuation.
  • Competitive Intensity (10%): Score is inversely proportional to competitive density. High barriers to entry yield higher scores.
  • Ease of Execution (10%): Accounts for development timelines, technical complexity, and regulatory permissions.
  • Capital Efficiency (5%): Favors asset-light operational models (SaaS) over capital-intensive setups (hardware acquisitions).
  • Risk Mitigation (5%): Mitigates financial, liability, legal, and operational risks.

2. Cross-Business Synergy Mechanics

Synergy A: Aequalis Global + JCS → HR Tech / Recruitment SaaS

Value Capture: Aequalis Global operates on transactional placement fees. By introducing a recruitment automation SaaS, JBG can charge mid-market clients a monthly subscription. JCS provides the software engineering, while Aequalis acts as the client channel.

Synergy B: Nature Grid + JCS → Solar Telemetry & Predictive O&M SaaS

Value Capture: Solar installation is a low-margin capital contract. Adding telemetry monitoring software lets Nature Grid sell long-term predictive maintenance and performance agreements, securing recurring revenue streams.

3. B2B Unit Economics Framework

Before launching a pilot, JBG must validate that the opportunity has a sustainable unit economic profile using three metrics:

Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

CAC = (Sales Costs + Marketing Costs) / (New Customers)

JBG can achieve near-zero marketing CAC by cross-selling to Aequalis and Nature Grid's existing corporate clients during initial launch phases.

Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

LTV = (Monthly ARPU × Gross Margin %) / Churn Rate

Subscription products must target an LTV to CAC ratio of greater than 3.0x for venture funding feasibility.
